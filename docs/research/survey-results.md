# Hokie Trade Survey Results

## Method
We used an online survey (Google Forms) shared with Virginia Tech students in September 2026. It asked how often students trade, what platforms they use, how much they trust strangers on general marketplace apps (1 to 5), whether they had a bad experience, how important VT-only verification is (1 to 5), which features they want, and whether they would use a VT-only app.

We got **12 responses**. 11 were current Virginia Tech students and 1 was not.

## Main Results

| Question | Result |
|---|---|
| Current VT students | 11 of 12 |
| Trade frequency | Occasionally 7, Sometimes 2, Never 3 |
| Trust in strangers on general apps (1 to 5) | Average 2.25 (range 1 to 3) |
| Ever had a bad experience | 0 of 12 |
| Importance of VT-only verification (1 to 5) | Average 3.6. 7 rated it 4 to 5, 4 rated it 1 to 2 |
| Likely to use a VT-only app | Yes/definitely 3, probably 8, probably not 1 |
| Biggest worry is scams or untrustworthy people | 8 of 12 |

## Features Respondents Wanted
(Multiple choices allowed.)

| Feature | Requested by |
|---|---|
| Buyer/seller ratings and reviews | 10 |
| In-app messaging | 9 |
| Verified @vt.edu accounts only | 9 |
| Suggested safe on-campus meetup spots | 9 |
| Service listings (tutoring, rides) | 5 |
| Push notifications for new listings | 4 |

## What We Learned
- Trust is the main barrier. Nobody had actually been scammed, but the fear alone was enough to keep students away from general marketplace apps.
- Because of this, ratings, verification, and safe meetup spots are part of our core product, not stretch goals.
- VT-only verification was valued, but not by everyone, so it should not be our only trust feature.

## Limits
Small sample, mostly people we know, and one respondent was not a VT student. We treat these results as guidance for a first version.
